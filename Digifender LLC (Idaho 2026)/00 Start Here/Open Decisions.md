## 1. Before signing the operating agreement

- **Initial contributions:** Ender will supply the initial cash and Whumpo-related assets as capital, with no payback unless he specifies an exception. Inventory each item and its current legal owner and value/basis. This does not change the 50/50 profit and distribution split.
- **Authority and approvals:** Both members will have Treasury access; Ender approves financial actions and monthly bookkeeping. Ender requests final authority over major business decisions. Draft precise voting and contract-authority terms, then compare them to the accepted Idaho governor record. At account opening, map the financial policy to actual Treasury roles and controls. Alexa's operating duties can be determined later.
- **Exit and succession:** Ender's proposed final authority over ordinary and major business choices must be written precisely and does not remove all statutory member rights. Decide how to value an interest and what happens if one owner leaves, becomes unable to work, or dies.
- **Ownership of work:** Digifender LLC is intended to own existing and future Whumpo code outright. Confirm the current legal title to code and related domains/branding/accounts, then complete the [IP assignment draft](../02%20Governance/Whumpo%20IP%20Assignment%20Draft.md) and any account transfers. Personal licensing is not planned absent a concrete tax/business reason. [U.S. Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap2.html)
- **Profit sharing and distributions:** Profits and cash distributions are 50/50; Ender decides whether and when the LLC makes a lawful distribution. The operating agreement draft uses 50/50 losses subject to required contributed-property tax allocations. A distribution is separate from an expense for bookkeeping.
