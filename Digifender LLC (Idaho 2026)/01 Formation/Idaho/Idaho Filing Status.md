## Once Idaho accepts the filing

1. Save the accepted Certificate of Organization here and record the entity number and formation date in this file and in `00 Start Here/README.md`.
2. Check the accepted record against the draft: name, registered agent (PhysicalAddress.com), addresses, and governors. Reconcile the governor listing with the operating agreement's authority terms.
3. Apply for the EIN using the [EIN worksheet](../../03%20Company%20Records/Tax%20IDs/EIN%20Application%20Worksheet.docx), with Ender as responsible party. Save the IRS confirmation in `03 Company Records/Tax IDs/`.
4. Open Stripe Treasury under the exact accepted name and EIN; save the account agreement in `04 Finance/`.
5. File the Whumpo assumed business name using the [ABN worksheet](Whumpo%20Assumed%20Business%20Name%20Worksheet.md) before transacting as Whumpo.
6. Finish and sign the [operating agreement](../../02%20Governance/Operating%20Agreement%20Draft.md).
7. Calendar the first Idaho annual report, due in the anniversary month of formation each year starting the year after formation. [Idaho Secretary of State business FAQ](https://sos.idaho.gov/business-resources/business-faq/)

If the state rejects or returns the filing, save the notice here and record the reason before refiling.
