## Date discrepancy to note

The state records a **filing date of 1 October 2026**, but the fee receipt is dated **29 October 2026**, and the certificate's margin stamp reads "Received by Office of the Idaho Secretary of State 10/29/2026 2:37 PM." The likely explanation is that the certificate was submitted and signed in SOSBiz on 1 October (which matches the owners' 1 October "filing pending" note) but the fee was only paid and the filing received for review on 29 October, and Idaho kept the original submission date as the filing date. Treat **1 October 2026** as the official formation date, because that is what the state record says. The acknowledgment asks the filer to report any discrepancy immediately. If 1 October is not the intended date, call the Secretary of State at (208) 334-2301.
