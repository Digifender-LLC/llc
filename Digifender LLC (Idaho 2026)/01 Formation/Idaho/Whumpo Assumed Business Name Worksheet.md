Idaho's ABN instructions say an entity transacting under a name other than its true legal name must file an assumed business name certificate before doing so. Check availability in SOSBiz, confirm the legal entity's accepted name and number, then complete and review the actual state form. An ABN does not create a separate LLC or trademark right. [Idaho Secretary of State ABN instructions](https://sos.idaho.gov/CORP/forms/ABN/ABN.pdf), [business FAQ](https://sos.idaho.gov/business-resources/business-faq/)


**Note, 9 November 2026:** the Idaho instructions quoted above say an entity transacting under a name other than its legal name must file. Whether a product name counts has not been confirmed.
