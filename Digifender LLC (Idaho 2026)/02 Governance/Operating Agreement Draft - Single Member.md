# Operating Agreement of Digifender LLC (single-member version)

It is a drafting aid, not legal advice. Have an Idaho attorney review it before signing, particularly the points under "Open points for the owner" at the end.

## 1. Company

1.1 **Formation.** Digifender LLC (the "Company") is an Idaho limited liability company. Idaho accepted its Certificate of Organization on 30 October 2026 under file number 7029055, with a filing (formation) date of 1 October 2026. Its status is Active-Existing and its duration is perpetual.

1.2 **Office and agent.** The principal office is 9169 W State St #884, Garden City, ID 83714. The registered agent is PHYSICALADDRESS.COM, LLC, 9169 W State St, Garden City, ID 83714-1733. A later change will be recorded as Idaho law requires.

1.3 **Purpose.** The Company may develop, own, operate and license software, AI, website and application products and services, including the product Whumpo, and may carry on any lawful business.

1.4 **Name of the product.** Whumpo is a product of the Company and is not a separate entity. The Company's legal name is "Digifender LLC." (See open point 4 about the Idaho assumed business name rule.)

1.5 **Term.** The Company continues until dissolved under this Agreement or Idaho law.

## 2. Member and ownership

2.1 **Sole member.** Ender Raven Solomon Kaina ("Ender" or the "Member") is the sole member and owns 100% of the Company's membership interest, including all economic and governance rights.

2.2 **Tax classification.** The Company is a single-member LLC. Unless the Member makes a valid election to be taxed otherwise, it is disregarded as separate from its owner for federal income tax purposes, and its income and expenses are reported on the Member's own return (typically Schedule C). The Company keeps its own EIN as recorded in the IRS notice (kept in the owners' private records, not in this repository).

2.3 **No other members.** No other person has any interest in the Company, profits, or distributions, or any right to become a member, unless Ender admits them by a signed written amendment.

## 3. Management

3.1 **Member-managed.** The Company is managed by its Member. Ender has full authority to act for the Company and to bind it, including to sign contracts, open and operate accounts, hire and engage contractors, borrow, buy and sell assets, set prices, adopt policies, and make distributions.

3.2 **Officers and agents.** Ender may appoint officers, agents or employees and delegate authority to them in a signed writing. A delegation does not make the delegate a member or governor and may be revoked at any time.

3.3 **Reliance.** A third party may rely on Ender's signature as the Company's signature.

## 4. Capital, distributions and records

4.1 **Contributions.** Ender may contribute cash, property or services at any time. Unless a signed or dated written record identifies a payment as a loan or a reimbursable expense, it is a capital contribution and the Company has no obligation to repay it. Schedule A lists initial contributions and assets. No additional contribution is required.

4.2 **Distributions.** Ender decides whether and when to make distributions, subject to the limits in Idaho Code § 30-25-405, which bars a distribution that would leave the Company unable to pay its debts as they come due.

4.3 **Separateness.** The Company keeps its own books and funds separate from Ender's personal and household finances, with a distinct financial account (Stripe Treasury). Record owner contributions, distributions, loans, fees and refunds with supporting statements and receipts. Pay Company expenses from Company funds where practical, and when Ender pays one personally, record Ender as the payer so it can be reimbursed or treated as a contribution under section 4.1. Where an expense is paid from a joint personal account, record Ender as payer and keep the account statement as support.

4.4 **Limited liability.** The Company's debts and obligations belong to the Company. Neither Ender nor any officer or agent is personally liable for them solely because of that role, to the extent Idaho law provides. This depends on following the Company's formalities and keeping its money separate, as in section 4.3.

4.5 **Indemnity.** To the extent Idaho law allows, the Company will indemnify Ender and its officers and agents against liability from acting for the Company in good faith, except for willful misconduct, knowing violation of law, or improper personal benefit. The Company may buy insurance for this.

## 5. Whumpo and other intellectual property

5.1 **Company ownership.** The Company will own Whumpo and its related code, domains, designs, content, marks and accounts, to the extent transferable. Ender will sign the assignments and provider transfers needed to vest each item in the Company; see the [Whumpo IP Assignment Draft](Whumpo%20IP%20Assignment%20Draft.md). Copyright transfers need a signed writing.

5.2 **Future work.** Work Ender creates for the Company belongs to the Company. Contractor work needs its own written ownership terms. Open-source and third-party license obligations continue to apply.

## 6. Continuity, transfer and dissolution

6.1 **Transfer.** Ender may transfer all or part of the interest and may admit new members by signed written amendment. If Ender transfers part, the Company's tax status will change and the Company should obtain tax advice first.

6.2 **Death or incapacity.** On Ender's death or incapacity, the Company continues. Ender's interest passes under his estate plan, and the person named in Schedule B (or, if none, his personal representative or agent under a power of attorney) may exercise Ender's rights and keep the Company operating until the interest is transferred. **[Ender to name the successor and backup, see open point 3.]**

6.3 **Dissolution.** The Company dissolves when Ender decides in writing, on administrative or judicial dissolution, or when Idaho law requires. On winding up, the Company pays its debts and distributes the rest to Ender.

## 7. General

7.1 **Amendment.** Only Ender may amend this Agreement, in a signed writing.

7.2 **Governing law.** Idaho law governs the Company's internal affairs, subject to nonwaivable rules (see Idaho Code § 30-25-105).

7.3 **Severability and entire agreement.** If a provision is invalid, the rest continues. This Agreement and its schedules are the whole agreement about the Company's governance and replace any earlier understanding, including the unsigned two-member working draft.

7.4 **Counterparts and electronic signature.** This Agreement may be signed electronically.

## Open points for the owner (not operative provisions)

1. **Single member or two members?** The existing workspace draft is 50/50 with Alexa as a member and Ender holding 51% of the votes. The IRS record, the Stripe and bookkeeping setup, and your current instructions point to a single-member LLC. Pick one. If Ender is the sole owner, use this draft and consider whether Alexa should be paid, employed or given an interest some other way. If the two of you are meant to co-own it, use the other draft and settle the tax classification (a spousal community property election under IRS Rev. Proc. 2002-69 would keep disregarded status; otherwise the IRS record would need to be corrected and the LLC would file as a partnership).
2. **Alexa is listed as a governor on the Idaho filing.** Under the Idaho LLC Act a governor is normally a member or manager. If Alexa is not a member, the Idaho record and this agreement do not match. Options: update the Idaho record, admit her as a member, or document her as a manager with limited delegated authority. Also consider whether a spouse acknowledgment is appropriate, because Idaho is a community property state and the startup funds came from a joint account. This is the main reason an attorney should look at the draft.
3. **Successor.** Pick who runs Whumpo if Ender is unavailable and complete Schedule B.
4. **Whumpo name.** The prior worksheet treated an Idaho assumed business name filing as needed before transacting as "Whumpo." You have decided no DBA is needed because Whumpo is a product of the Company. Idaho's instructions say an entity doing business under a name other than its legal name must file one, and I could not confirm that a product name is exempt. Confirm this with the Secretary of State or an attorney before the first paid sale.
5. **Schedule A** needs the actual figures and asset list.
