# Whumpo intellectual property assignment - drafting worksheet

**Prepared:** 1 October 2026  
**Status:** Unexecuted draft. This document does not transfer any rights until the LLC exists, the schedules are completed, and the actual owners sign an operative assignment.

## Intended transaction

The members have decided that Digifender LLC will own outright the Whumpo software created before and after formation, including copyright and other transferable rights held by the contributing owners. Ender Raven Solomon Kaina will treat the startup transfer as capital, without repayment, unless he specifies a written exception. Because the owners are married and claim Texas domicile, confirm whether any relevant asset is community or separate property before identifying the assignor or crediting a member's capital account. Both spouses can sign to transfer any rights each actually holds, but a signature cannot transfer a third party's rights. [Texas Family Code § 3.002](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/FA/htm/FA.3.htm#3.002)

## Proposed operative points for counsel/final draft

1. Each actual owner assigns to Digifender LLC all of that owner's transferable right, title and interest in the identified Whumpo assets, including the right to use, modify, distribute, license and enforce copyright in assigned code, subject to disclosed third-party rights.
2. The assignors deliver repository and account-control information through secure means; no credentials belong in this document or chat.
3. The assignment identifies each asset precisely enough to distinguish existing code and files from future work. The operating agreement or a separate invention-assignment clause covers future member-created work for Digifender.
4. The company records the contribution and its tax basis separately from the 50/50 economic ownership split. It does not assume the market value of founder time is tax basis. [IRS Publication 551](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p551)
5. Any asset that cannot legally or contractually be transferred remains listed as an exception with a separately approved migration or license plan.

Copyright ownership transfers generally require a writing signed by the owner or authorized agent. This worksheet is not that signed instrument. [17 USC § 204](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap2.html)
