## 2. Members and economic interests

2.1 **Members.** The members are Ender Raven Solomon Kaina (“Ender”) and Alexa Faith Kaina (“Alexa”). Each holds **50% of the Company's economic interest**. Their contributions may differ without changing that percentage.

2.2 **Profits and losses.** Company profits and losses are allocated 50% to Ender and 50% to Alexa, except for any special tax allocation required for contributed property or other mandatory tax rule. The Company will maintain separate capital and tax-basis records for each member; equal economic percentages do not imply equal capital account balances.

2.3 **Distributions.** Any cash distribution approved under this Agreement will be paid 50% to Ender and 50% to Alexa at the same time. This intended equal split includes net proceeds remaining after Company debts in a lawful winding up; Ender has no preferential return of contributed capital. Ender decides whether and when the Company proposes an interim distribution, subject to the Company's ability to pay its debts and Idaho's distribution limits. No member has a right to demand an interim distribution merely because the Company has taxable income.

2.4 **Economic-rights protection.** A change to the 50/50 ownership, profit/loss allocation, or distribution percentages requires a written amendment signed by both members. Neither member may dilute the other's economic interest by unilateral action.

## 3. Capital and member payments

3.1 **Initial capital.** Ender intends to supply the Company's initial money and Whumpo-related assets. Schedule A will identify each item, its source, date, agreed accounting treatment, and supporting receipt or transfer document. No present-dollar amount or asset value is represented until the schedule is completed. Alexa has no initial payment obligation merely because she owns 50%.

3.2 **No automatic repayment.** Ender's initial and later accepted contributions are capital contributions and are not debts the Company must repay. A particular payment will be treated as a loan or reimbursable expense only if Ender identifies it as an exception in a separate written record accepted by the Company. To the extent Idaho law allows, this provision is intended to replace the default treatment of an advance beyond previously agreed capital under Idaho Code § 30-25-407(f)-(g). Personal payments made before formation must be reviewed and classified when the books are opened; this clause does not by itself make a cost tax deductible.

3.3 **No open-ended funding promise.** Neither member is obligated to contribute additional cash, property, or services after signing this Agreement unless that member expressly agrees to a specific contribution in a signed record. An additional voluntary payment is presumed to be capital when knowingly accepted by the Company unless a contemporaneous written record identifies it as a loan or reimbursable expense. Record it promptly in the books with its source and supporting evidence.

3.4 **No automatic ownership change.** A later contribution by one member does not change the 50/50 economic split unless both sign an amendment.

## 4. Management and voting

4.1 **Member-managed company.** The Company is member-managed. Both members are governors for Idaho filing purposes. This structure is intended to match the pending Certificate of Organization draft naming both members. It must be checked against the accepted filing before execution.

4.2 **Decision authority.** Ender is the lead managing member and has final authority over the Company's ordinary and major business decisions to the fullest extent an operating agreement may provide under Idaho law. This includes product strategy, Whumpo development and launch, pricing, customer and vendor contracts, hiring and contractor engagement, banking, borrowing, asset purchases and sales, and whether to make lawful distributions. Alexa may participate in discussion and perform duties delegated later, but an ordinary disagreement within Ender's authority is resolved by Ender's decision.

4.3 **Voting rule.** Solely for Company governance, Ender holds 51% of the member voting power and Alexa holds 49%. This voting allocation does **not** change their 50/50 ownership of profits, losses and distributions. An act needing member approval under a default majority rule may be approved by Ender. An act that Idaho law permits this Agreement to remove from a default unanimity rule may likewise be approved by Ender under this section. These terms do not override the reserved matters in section 4.4 or a nonwaivable legal requirement.

4.4 **Reserved matters.** Both members must sign: (a) any amendment to this Agreement; (b) any change to a member's economic percentage or a required contribution obligation; and (c) any transaction for which Idaho law preserves each member's personal approval right despite an operating agreement, including a merger, interest exchange, conversion or domestication to the extent applicable. Nothing in this Agreement eliminates a member's legally protected information rights, required duties of good faith or care, or the right to challenge unlawful conduct.

4.5 **Authority to bind the Company.** Ender may sign and direct Company transactions within section 4.2. Alexa may bind the Company only within a delegation recorded in writing by Ender or with Ender's contemporaneous written approval, except where applicable law provides otherwise. Both members may view Company financial records and other information to which they are entitled.

4.6 **Later roles.** Alexa's work responsibilities, hours, job title, or compensation are not fixed by this Agreement. A later written role assignment does not alter her 50% economic interest. Any compensation arrangement is documented separately and reviewed for tax treatment.

## 5. Banking, records and tax administration

5.1 **Financial account.** Stripe Treasury is the planned Company financial account. Both members may receive account access. Ender is the financial approver. The account's actual user roles, permissions, and approval workflow must be configured and tested when opened; this Agreement alone does not configure Stripe or Adminifender.

5.2 **Books.** Keep Company and household finances separate. Record gross customer receipts, processor fees, refunds, owner contributions, distributions, assets and liabilities, with original statements and receipts. Ender reviews and signs off on each monthly close. The Company may use the existing Digifender bookkeeping workbook while volume and complexity remain manageable.

5.3 **Tax status and year.** Unless a valid election changes its classification, the two-member Company will use the default federal partnership classification. The intended tax year is the calendar year, subject to applicable tax rules. The Company will select its partnership representative when preparing the first required federal partnership return.

## 6. Whumpo intellectual property

6.1 **Outright Company ownership.** The members intend Digifender LLC to own Whumpo software code and transferable related rights outright. Personal licensing of the code to the Company is not the agreed structure.

6.2 **Existing assets.** The actual current owner or owners of pre-formation code, domains, branding, designs and other assets must be identified in Schedule A. An owner will execute a separate signed assignment or provider transfer needed to vest each transferable asset in the Company. This Agreement does not claim rights held by a third party or itself transfer an asset not adequately identified and owned by a signer.

6.3 **Future work.** Each member agrees that work created for the Company within assigned Company duties is for the Company's benefit and will execute reasonable further documents to vest transferable rights in the Company. Contractor-created work must have its own written ownership terms. Existing open-source and third-party license obligations remain in force.

## 7. Transfers, departure and continuity

7.1 **No automatic management transfer.** A transfer of an economic interest alone does not automatically give the recipient management authority, except as required by law.

7.2 **Open succession terms.** **[Complete before signing:]** Define what happens if either member dies, becomes incapacitated, divorces, wants to leave, or wishes to sell their interest; whether the Company or the other member has a purchase right; how an interest is valued and paid for; and who may operate Whumpo when Ender is unavailable. Until agreed, the applicable Idaho statutory rules govern matters not covered here.

## 8. General terms

8.1 **Amendment.** An amendment must be in writing and signed by both members.

8.2 **Applicable law.** Idaho law governs the internal affairs of the Company, subject to applicable federal law and other nonwaivable rules.

8.3 **Severability.** If a provision is invalid, the remaining provisions continue to the extent lawful and consistent with the members' 50/50 economic bargain.

8.4 **Entire agreement.** When signed, this Agreement and its completed schedules supersede earlier oral or written member understandings about the Company's internal governance. It does not replace a separately required asset assignment, customer agreement, or filing.
