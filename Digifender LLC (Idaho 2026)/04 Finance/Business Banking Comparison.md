## Mercury versus Stripe Treasury: decision factors

The owners report both options are obtainable, so access is no longer the deciding factor for this comparison. Stripe's direct Treasury product is distinct from Treasury for platforms. Digifender selected the direct product as its planned financial account. [Treasury](https://stripe.com/treasury)

Treasury combines Stripe customer payments, operating funds, card spending, and reporting in one dashboard. Stripe currently advertises 2% cashback on eligible Treasury card purchases, although the expected low business spend makes rewards a minor factor. Some transfers and payouts can incur fees. [Treasury](https://stripe.com/treasury), [pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing)

Mercury offers a separate operating account with clear CSV/XLSX exports and a transactions API. Standard domestic ACH and wires are free. [Plans](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28769703794580-Mercury-pricing-plans), [exports](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28768700685844-Bulk-export-statements-receipts-and-CSVs-from-Mercury-Business)

Stripe Payments can pay out to an external bank account, so either operating-account choice permits Stripe customer billing. For a low-volume business, use exported statements/transactions for monthly reconciliation in the existing workbook and require human approval for money movement. [Stripe payouts](https://docs.stripe.com/payouts)
