A D-U-N-S number is a public business identifier that Dun & Bradstreet lists in its directory, so it is safe to keep in this public repository. It is not a tax ID; the EIN stays in the owners' private records.

Common uses: Apple Developer Program enrollment as an organization, Google Play organization accounts, and vendor or supplier applications that ask for one. Check the D&B listing for the legal name, address and phone number before using it, since app stores verify against it.
